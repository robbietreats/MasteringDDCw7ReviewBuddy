Rounding rules used in every question

  1. Round only at the final step. Keep every digit through the setup; the calculator chains the whole problem.
  2. Follow the question's instruction (nearest whole, tenth or hundredth). Look at the next digit: 5 or more rounds up.
  3. Tablets: give the calculated amount (½ tablet only if scored). When a question says "nearest whole number," 2.5 → 3.
  4. No instruction given: answers are shown exactly (e.g., 3.75 mL) and the tenth (3.8 mL) is also accepted.
  5. Leading zero, no trailing zero: write 0.5 mL, never .5 mL; write 5 mL, not 5.0 mL, unless tenths or hundredths are asked (5.0, 1.20).
  6. Weight: lb ÷ 2.2 = kg. Keep full precision until the end.

Your notes list conversions only, so these rounding rules are standard DDC conventions from your challenge decks. Follow your instructor if they differ.

24-hour clock

AM (inner ring): 0100–1159. Add a leading zero: 7:15 AM = 0715.

PM (outer ring): add 12 to the hour: 6:30 PM = 1830.

Back to standard: hour 13 or more → subtract 12, PM. 2145 = 9:45 PM.

Midnight = 0000 (or 2400) · Noon = 1200. Past 2400, subtract 24: 2230 + 4 h = 0230.
